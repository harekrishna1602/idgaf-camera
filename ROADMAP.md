- **Phase 1 (`v0.1.0`) — DONE:** Native Camera2 Engine, Multi-Lens Switcher, 1080P/4K & 24/30/60 FPS, Timecode, Tap-to-Focus.
- **Phase 2 (`v0.2.0`) — DONE:** Full Manual ISO, Shutter Speed + 180° Rule, Manual Focus + A/B Rack Focus Pull, Kelvin White Balance, AE/AF Lock, Anti-Burst Record Exposure Fix.
- **Phase 3 (`v0.3.0`) — DONE:** Cinema Monitoring Overlays (Focus Peaking, Zebras, Aspect Ratio Guides `2.39:1 / 16:9 / 9:16`, Rule of Thirds + Crosshair, Live Luma Histogram) + Fixed REC Shutter Inner Square Scaling.

  - Silent GitHub Releases **In-App Updater**.
- **Phase 6 (`v0.6.0`) — Logged Notes:**
  - Full Portrait + Landscape auto-rotation support (HUD rotates cleanly with phone orientation).
  - In-app **Clip Gallery / Media Browser** (browse, play, scrub, share, and delete *all* recorded clips in `Movies/IDGAF_Camera`, not just the last clip).
  - Smooth **Pinch-to-Zoom & Cinema Zoom Rocker** (`CONTROL_ZOOM_RATIO` / `SCALER_CROP_REGION`).
