# IDGAF CAMERA (`v0.6.5` — EV Compensation, Horizon Level & Self-Timer)

Free, open, community-built Android cinema camera built on native **Kotlin + Camera2 API** by **Studio Mogli** ([www.studiomogli.com](https://www.studiomogli.com)).

## What's New in `v0.6.5`
- **EV Exposure Compensation (`EV`):** Dedicated pill in the bottom cinema dock to brighten or darken Auto Exposure in real time (`-2.0` to `+2.0` stops, clamped to your camera's hardware EV range) with 1-tap `0.0` reset.
- **Live Horizon Level (`MON → LEVEL`):** Gyro/gravity-powered center horizon line that turns neon green (`#34C759`) when your phone is dead level (`±1.5°`) and Safety Orange when tilted.
- **Record Self-Timer (`PRO → TMR: OFF / 3s / 5s`):** Big center countdown overlay before rolling—tap the shutter again during countdown to cancel.
