# IDGAF CAMERA (`v0.7.3` — 3-Workbench Switcher & Era 1: 1920s Silent 35mm)

*A love letter to indie filmmakers — by Savior San.*

Free, open Android cinema camera powered by the **Savior Cinema Engine** (native **Kotlin + Camera2 + OpenGL ES 2.0**) by **Studio Mogli** ([www.studiomogli.com](https://www.studiomogli.com)).

## What's New in `v0.7.3`
- **3-Workbench Architecture (`SAVIOR` / `ERA` / `PURIST [Phase 8]`):**
  - Tap **`ERA`** in the top bar (or long-press for the Workbench Switcher) to switch **instantaneously with zero camera reboot** into the Vintage Camera Museum.
  - Entering **`ERA`** hides **100% of modern controls** (Audio VU, `LOOK`, `PRO`, `MON`, Zoom Rocker, Pinch-to-Zoom, `PILOT`, and modern telemetry dock) and snapshots your full `SAVIOR` rig so returning restores everything cleanly.
- **Era 1 — 1920s Silent 35mm (*Bell & Howell / Hand-Crank Era*):**
  - **Authentic Controls Only:** `CRANK` (`16 FPS` / `18 FPS`), mechanical **`IRIS`** (relative f-stops `f/2.0`–`f/16`), **`FOCUS`** ring (`INF ∞`–`MACRO` + `AF`), **`PRINT`** (`NITRATE` fresh print ↔ `ARCHIVE` aged vault), **`⇄ LENS`** flip, and live **35mm Footage Counter (`000 FT • 00:00` at 16 frames/ft)**.
  - **Genuine 1920s GPU Optical Pipeline (Preview + MP4):** Baked **`1.33:1` rounded brass aperture gate**, red-blind **Orthochromatic B&W** (dark velvety reds, bright luminous skies), high-contrast nitrate S-curve, subtle warm silver-nitrate tint, hand-crank shutter exposure flicker, `mediump`-safe animated 35mm grain, optical Petzval corner vignette, and archival vertical hairline scratches & dust in `ARCHIVE` mode.
  - **Authentic Silent Recording:** Saves video-only `.mp4` clips at true `16 FPS` or `18 FPS` (`IDGAF_1920s_16fps_*.mp4`).
