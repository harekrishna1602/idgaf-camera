# IDGAF CAMERA (`v0.7.6` — Era 2: 1950s Monochrome 35mm, Locked Era 1080P, Rotated 9:16 Portrait Cue & Custom Self-Timer)

*A love letter to indie filmmakers — by Savior San.*

Free, open Android cinema camera powered by the **Savior Cinema Engine** (native **Kotlin + Camera2 + OpenGL ES 2.0**) by **Studio Mogli** ([www.studiomogli.com](https://www.studiomogli.com)).

## What's New in `v0.7.6`
- **Era 2 — 1950s Monochrome 35mm (Satyajit Ray / Kurosawa / Bergman Optical & Sound Engine):**
  - **Mitchell BNC Crystal-Sync 24 FPS + Dual Gates:** Switch inline between classic **`1.37` Academy** (`1.375:1`) and **`1.85` Widescreen Flat** (`1.85:1`) with razor-sharp 90° cinema aperture mattes.
  - **5 Real Optical Wratten Glass Contrast Filters (`FILTER`):** **`CLEAR`** (Eastman Plus-X / Double-X Panchromatic), **`Y8`** (Yellow — cuts blue sky haze, luminous skin), **`O21`** (Orange — Kurosawa dramatic skies), **`R25`** (Red — Bergman / Film Noir black skies), and **`G11`** (Green — studio portrait skin & foliage).
  - **Single-Coated Cooke Speed Panchro Multi-Pass Optics:** Crisp resolving center, tight silver-halide specular halation (`knee 0.78`, `radius 2.2%`), Zone III–VII silver-gelatin tonal curve (velvety D-max blacks & neutral-warm baryta highlights), and fine tri-acetate 35mm safety grain scaling with **`ASA 50 / 100 / 250 / 400`** + 1-tap **`LOCK 180°` (`1/48s`)**.
  - **Authentic Westrex RA-1231 Optical Mono Sound-on-Film:** Lossless-video MP4 remux with Academy Curve HPF, RCA 44BX ribbon mic chest warmth, 24 dB/oct optical slit-loss HF roll-off, optical light-valve leveling, vacuum-tube harmonics, and **`STUDIO / CRITERION / ARCHIVE`** print wear.
- **Locked Native Era Resolution (`1080P`):** Entering `ERA` snapshots SAVIOR's resolution/codec/bitrate and locks 35mm film cameras to native **`1080P @ 24 Mbps H.264`**, restoring SAVIOR's exact format when you switch back.
- **Rotated 90° Physical Grip Cue for `9:16 PORT`:** Both the `9:16 FULL PORTRAIT` confirmation modal and the live side-edge `9:16 PORTRAIT • UPRIGHT` badge rotate ±90° toward the earpiece so they read upright while holding the phone vertically and never overlap top alerts.
- **Customizable Self-Timer (`PRO → TMR`):** Inline **`OFF | 3s | 5s | 10s | CUSTOM`** (`1–60s`) drawer inside `PRO`.
